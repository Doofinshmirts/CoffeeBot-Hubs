# CoffeeBot demonstration and recording

Use two phones or two independent browser sessions, one as community visitor and one as driver. Use a clearly labeled demonstration hub and test grounds. Hide credentials and keys from the recording.

1. Show the community page and the starting available quantity.
2. Show the empty container being tared on the physical scale.
3. Weigh grounds. Enter the actual weight; use 5 kg only if the scale actually reads 5 kg. For a software-only example, explicitly label 5 kg as simulated.
4. Select the hub, enter rider/source, and capture a photo showing grounds and the reading.
5. Submit and show the saved confirmation and timestamp.
6. Keep the community page visible. Show availability increasing and the new-batch alert within the next polling interval.
7. Open hub details and explain the location/instructions.
8. After a simulated full collection, have staff mark the batch collected. Show availability decreasing.
9. Explain that alerts need the webpage open and that the scale value is manually entered.

Record a 60-90 second software walkthrough and keep a full physical rehearsal separately. The recording generated by tests/browser_check.py is fixture evidence only and must not be presented as proof of a Supabase or real-scale integration.
