# CoffeeBot demonstration and recording

Use two phones or two independent browser sessions, one as a registered community user and one as the driver. Use a clearly labelled cardboard demonstration hub and test grounds. Hide credentials, personal details, and keys from the recording.

1. Show the public hub list and starting available quantity.
2. As the driver, sign in, choose the same hub, and press **Open lid**. Physically lift the cardboard lid while explaining that the on-screen lock and scale are simulated.
3. Enter the rider/source, deposited weight, and a photo of the grounds/readout. Submit; show that the software locks the lid, stops the scale, and raises availability.
4. In the second session, sign in with a pre-confirmed individual or organization account and select the same hub.
5. Press **Open lid**. Point out the starting weight and the five-minute access window, then physically remove demonstration grounds.
6. Enter the new remaining weight and take a photo. Before submitting, show the automatically calculated kilograms taken.
7. Submit; show the collection receipt, locked-lid state, and reduced public availability.
8. Explain that the database records the account, hub, before/after weights, calculated amount, time, and private evidence photo.
9. State the limitation accurately: the cardboard lid and scale are not electronically connected; the website demonstrates the intended control journey.

Record a 60-90 second software walkthrough and keep a full physical rehearsal separately. Pre-confirm the community email before presenting. The recording generated by `tests/browser_check.py` is fixture evidence only and must not be presented as proof of a Supabase or real-scale integration.
